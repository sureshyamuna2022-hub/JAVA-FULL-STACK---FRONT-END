Welocme To Sola System:

1 Mercury:

Mercury is the first and smallest planet in the Solar System. It is the closest planet to the Sun and completes one orbit around the Sun in just 88 Earth days.

Mercury is a rocky planet with many craters on its surface, similar to the Moon. It has very high temperatures during the day and very cold temperatures at night because it has a very thin atmosphere.

2 Venus:

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is often called Earth’s “sister planet” because it is similar in size to Earth.

Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System because of its thick atmosphere, which traps heat. It is covered with clouds and has a rocky surface.

3 Earth:

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life. It has air, water, and a suitable temperature for living things.

About 71% of Earth is covered with water, which is why it is called the “Blue Planet.” Earth has one natural satellite called the Moon.

4 Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is known as the “Red Planet” because of its reddish surface caused by iron oxide (rust).

Mars is a rocky planet with mountains, valleys, and deserts. Scientists study Mars to learn whether life may have existed there in the past.

5 Jupiter:

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in the Solar System. It is a giant gas planet mainly made of hydrogen and helium.

Jupiter is famous for its Great Red Spot, which is a huge storm larger than Earth. It also has many moons and faint rings.

6 Saturn:

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is best known for its beautiful rings made of ice and rock particles.

Saturn is a gas giant planet mainly made of hydrogen and helium. It is the second-largest planet in the Solar System and has many moons.

7 Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant planet with a pale blue-green color caused by methane gas in its atmosphere.

Uranus rotates on its side, which makes it different from other planets. It is very cold and has faint rings and many moons.

8 Neptune:

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun in the Solar System. It is a dark blue ice giant planet known for its very strong winds.

Neptune has a cold atmosphere made mainly of hydrogen, helium, and methane. It also has rings and several moons.
